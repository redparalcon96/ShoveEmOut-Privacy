01Scope and controller

This policy explains what information is used by ShoveEmOut , why it is used, and the choices available to you. It applies to the Android version developed and published by RedParalGames.

For purposes of applicable data protection law, RedParalGames is the controller of the processing described in this policy. Google processes data for its own services under Google’s privacy policy.

02Data stored on your device

The Game stores progress, coins, unlocked equipment, purchased extra lives, settings and daily-spin state locally on your device. It does not require an account and does not send your local save data to RedParalGames.

You can remove local data by clearing the Game’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the Game. Uninstalling may permanently remove progress and consumable items. Purchase records remain with Google Play.

03Advertising and consent

The Game uses Google AdMob to show interstitial and rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as:

  • Device advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP
  • Device and app information
  • Ad interactions
  • Diagnostic information

This information is used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve advertising services. Google may process data in countries other than yours under its own safeguards. RedParalGames does not receive your name, email address or precise location from AdMob.

Where required by law, the Game asks for consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) before using personal data for personalized advertising. You can decline. The Game remains fully playable; you may receive non-personalized ads or no ads, depending on your choice and ad availability. Even when ads are non-personalized, Google may process limited data for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and measurement as described in its policy.

Your choice can change. Open Settings > Privacy in the Game to review, change or withdraw your ad consent. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID and manage ad personalization in Android settings.

Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
Google partner-sites information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

04Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment method and billing details; RedParalGames does not receive your card number or full billing address. RedParalGames receives the product identifier and purchase confirmation needed to grant coins or extra lives.

Refunds and billing disputes are handled under Google Play’s policies.

05Service providers and disclosure

We use Google AdMob and Google Play Billing, which process information under their own privacy policies. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the security of the Game, or as part of a business transfer.

The Game has no account system, and RedParalGames does not receive your name or email address from AdMob.

06Your choices and rights

  • Change or withdraw your ad consent in the Game’s Settings > Privacy screen.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID and manage ad personalization in Android settings.
  • Clear the Game’s storage or uninstall it to remove local data.
  • Contact us at contactredparal@gmail.com with a privacy question or request.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because the Game has no account, we may not be able to identify you in our systems; we will explain what we can do with the information available.

07Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

08Security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect information under our control. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Local game data remains on your device until you clear the Game’s storage or uninstall it. Google retains advertising and purchase data under its own policies. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to respond and meet legal obligations.

09Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date at the top of this page. The current version will remain available at this URL.
